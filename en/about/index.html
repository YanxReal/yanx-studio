Our story

From a two-person freelance partnership to a studio trusted by companies across three continents.

  1. 2019

    The first commit

    Yanx Studio starts as a two-person freelance partnership, shipping websites and internal tools for local businesses.

  2. 2021

    First full products

    We move from client work to end-to-end product development, launching our first fintech dashboard and telemedicine app.

  3. 2023

    A design-driven studio

    Product design joins engineering as a first-class discipline. Our first design system ships and conversion rates across client projects jump measurably.

  4. 2025

    Three continents

    Today we build web, mobile and desktop products for clients in the Americas and Europe — still senior-led.

  5. 2026

    International expansion

    From our United States base we open our calendar to international clients, adding desktop and mobile apps and AI-powered products to our toolkit.
